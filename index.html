        How to play
        • Name as many players as you can. Every player who fits scores points: 1 for a one-circle zone, 3 for a two-circle overlap, 10 for the center.
        • Get at least one player into all seven zones for a 10-point full-board bonus.
        • You have 45 seconds, starting with your first guess, and every player who fits a circle resets the clock to 45. A player who fits no circle is a miss, and three misses also end the game.
        • Typos get corrected, and a first or last name is enough. Names the game can't find don't count as a miss.
        • A new board appears every day at midnight your time. Use the arrows to play earlier days.
